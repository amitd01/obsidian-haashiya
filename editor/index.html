        Send text to Anthropic?

        AI features use Anthropic (Claude) with your own settings. When you use them, Margin sends to Anthropic:

        • AI alternates — the paragraph you're working on
        • Trim, Checks and ReduceSlop — the whole document (without ghosted text or overflow)

        Nothing is sent unless you use one of these. What happens to it there is set by that provider's privacy policy. You're asked again for each provider; removing its key in Settings withdraws this permission.
